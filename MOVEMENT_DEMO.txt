At sunrise in the royal palace, Prince Arjun buckled on his sword and shield, then waved goodbye to Queen Sita.

At the village, a storm broke over the rooftops. The villagers hurried indoors while the farmer pulled a cart through the muddy road.

At the riverbank, a fisherman clung to a small boat as the current carried it away. Hanuman ran along the shore, threw a rope to the fisherman, and pulled the boat toward safety. The fisherman climbed aboard and stood upright as the boat drifted to the bank.

In the forest, a fallen branch trapped a rabbit. Hanuman knelt beside it, lifted the heavy branch, and helped the rabbit hop free. A deer trotted through the trees while a lion walked nearby; a parrot flew overhead. Hanuman gave the tired rabbit a packet of medicine, and the animals gathered around.

At the battlefield, Arjun raised his shield as the Demon attacked. Arjun struck with his sword, the Demon fell, and then slowly got back up. Hanuman leapt between them and persuaded them to stop fighting.

At the royal palace, Arjun mounted his horse and rode home. The horse galloped beneath the evening sky. At night, the crescent moon shone above the courtyard as Hanuman, Sita, Arjun, and the villagers danced together.
